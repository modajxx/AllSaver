Privacy Policy

AllSaver — Download from any social media

Last updated: June 2, 2026

🛡️ Our Promise

AllSaver is built with privacy at its core. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no logins, no tracking, no analytics SDKs.

Short version: Whatever you download stays on your device. We never see your links, your media, or your activity.

📊 Data We Collect

None. AllSaver has no servers, no database, no user accounts. We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files outside the app
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • The links you paste or the media you download

🔓 Permissions We Use

AllSaver requests the following Android permissions strictly for app functionality:

  • Internet — to fetch media from public social media URLs you provide
  • Storage / Media access — to save downloaded files to your device gallery
  • Notifications — to show download progress
  • Foreground service — to keep downloads running when the app is in the background

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit personal data.

🌐 Third-Party Services

To extract media from social media links, AllSaver may use the open-source Cobalt extraction service. Cobalt is a privacy-respecting, MIT-licensed project that does not require login or store user data.

For some platforms, AllSaver may also directly call public APIs (such as Twitter/X's public syndication API). These calls contain only the URL you provided — no personal information.

📲 Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored locally on your device and never transmitted:

  • Downloaded media files (in your gallery)
  • Download history (in app's local database)
  • Your settings (language, theme, default quality)
  • Private vault PIN (encrypted, never leaves device)

You can clear this data anytime by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from your phone's Settings.

👶 Children's Privacy

AllSaver is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

⚖️ Your Responsibility

AllSaver is a tool. You are responsible for the content you download. Please respect copyright laws, platform terms of service, and the rights of content creators. Do not use AllSaver to download content you do not have permission to save.

🔄 Policy Updates

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of AllSaver after changes means you accept the updated policy.
